LabelCheck: terms of use and disclaimer

Template for the pilot. Have it reviewed by your lawyer before commercial use.

1. What the service does

LabelCheck reads label images, PDF artwork and e-commerce listings, and compares the text it reads with selected labelling rules (Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 as amended, and for food, the FSSAI Labelling and Display Regulations, 2020). The rule set version is shown on every result and report.

2. Not legal advice, no certificate

Results are produced automatically and can be wrong: text can be misread or missed, measurements are approximate, and some rules need human judgement. A result of "no issues found" does not mean a label complies with the law. LabelCheck does not certify compliance and is not a substitute for a qualified person or for advice from a lawyer or the Legal Metrology / FSSAI authorities.

3. Your responsibility

You remain fully responsible for your labels and listings. A qualified person in your organisation must review every result before a label is printed or a product is listed. Approvals recorded in LabelCheck are your organisation's decisions.

4. Rule coverage

Only the rules listed in the rule set are checked. Laws change; there may be a delay before changes are added. Rules that are not yet verified are shown as advisory and never as a hard failure.

5. Your data

Uploaded files, results and comments belong to your organisation and are visible only to its users. Every action is recorded in an audit log. We do not use your files to train models without written permission. Data is stored in the deployment region chosen by your organisation; backups are kept as configured by the operator.

6. E-commerce listings

When you provide listing text, LabelCheck checks the declarations in that text against the label. LabelCheck does not perform automated scraping of third-party marketplace websites.

7. Limitation of liability

To the extent permitted by law, the provider is not liable for losses arising from reliance on results, including penalties, recalls or reprinting costs. (Exact wording to be set in the commercial agreement.)
