Why Automated Compliance?

Navigate Complex Packaging Regulations Without Risk

Under the Legal Metrology Act and Food Safety and Standards Regulations, even a 0.5 mm error in numeral height or a missing consumer complaint email can halt shipments and lead to heavy financial penalties.

⚖️

Legal Metrology (PCR) 2011

Validates complete manufacturer/packer/importer declarations, PIN codes (Rule 10), Unit Sale Price formatting (Rule 6(11)), non-exaggerating net quantity words (Rule 12(6)), and principal display panel font sizes (Rule 7(2) Table-I).

🥗

FSSAI Regulations 2020 (v8)

Deep checks for food products: 14-digit FoSCoS licence format, mandatory Veg/Non-Veg symbol geometry and placement, %RDA nutritional tables, allergen callouts, and lot/batch code declarations.

🛒

E-Commerce Rule 6(10)

Cross-references e-commerce marketplace listings against physical label text to guarantee identical consumer declarations across retail shelves and online catalogs.
