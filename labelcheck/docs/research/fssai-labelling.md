# FSSAI Labelling & Display Regulations 2020 - research notes (2 Nov 2026)

Status codes: OFF = read in official FSSAI text (via web-fetch excerpts; re-check quotes against the PDF
before hard-coding strings), SEC = secondary only, UNV = unverified.
Main source: FSSAI compendium Version VIII (09.09.2025)

## Mandatory (Reg 5) - OFF unless noted
- 4(5) English or Hindi (Devanagari).
- 5(1) Name of the food indicating true nature.
- 5(2) Ingredients in descending order by weight/volume (not for single-ingredient foods); added water declared;
  QUID % when ingredient emphasised by words/pictures.
- 5(3) Nutrition per 100 g / 100 ml (or per single consumption pack) AND per-serve %RDA (2000 kcal basis):
  energy kcal, protein g, carbohydrate g, total sugars g, added sugars g, total fat g, saturated fat g,
  trans fat g, cholesterol mg (animal fat products), sodium mg. Exempt (5(3)(c)): unprocessed single-ingredient,
  drinking water, salt, table-top sweeteners, tea, coffee, vinegar, flavourings/additives, chewing gum, alcohol...
- 5(4) Veg: green filled circle in square with green outline; Non-veg: brown filled triangle in square with brown
  outline; on PDP close to name/brand. Sizes by PDP area (cm2): <=100: circle 3, triangle 2.5, square 6 mm;
  100-500: 4/3.5/8; 500-2500: 6/5/12; >2500: 8/7/16.
- 5(5) Additives: functional class + specific name or INS number.
- 5(6) Name and complete address of brand owner with "Manufactured by / Marketed by / Packed by"; importer for imports.
- 5(7) FSSAI logo and licence number of brand owner, contrasting colour. 14-digit format: from Licensing regs (UNV here).
- 5(8) Net qty, MRP, consumer care: Legal Metrology.
- 5(9) Batch / code / lot number.
- 5(10)(a) "Date of manufacture or packaging" and "Expiry/Use by" mandatory; "Best before" optional only.
  5(10)(b) shelf life <=3 months: DD/MM/YY; else month+year (month in capitals, >=3 letters) or DD/MM/YY.
  Expiry exempt: fresh fruit/veg, wine, alcohol >=10%, vinegar, boiled sugar confectionery, industrial salt,
  solid sugars, chewing gum.
- 5(11)/(12) Country of origin for imported food.
- 5(13) Instructions for use where applicable (warn only).
- 5(14) Allergens declared separately as "Contains ...": gluten cereals, crustaceans, milk, egg, fish, peanuts,
  tree nuts, soy, sulphite >=10 mg/kg. "May contain" for cross-contact. No bold-font rule.
- 6(3) Table I minimum font (in force 1 Jul 2022): PDP <=200 cm2: 1 mm (blown/moulded 2); 200-500: 2 (4);
  500-2500: 3 (5); >2500: 6 (8). Width >= 1/3 height.
- 8(1) Packs <=100 cm2 exempt from ingredients, lot, nutrition, additives, licence no. (+logo), importer details,
  instructions. 8(1)(a): date of manufacture not required < 30 cm2.
- Schedule II boxed warnings e.g. "CONTAINS CAFFEINE", polyols, aspartame, "contains added MONOSODIUM GLUTAMATE".

## Amendments
- 08.08.2025 coffee-chicory % (final, from 1 Jul 2026). OFF.
- 24.03.2026 (final, from 1 Jul 2027): infant %RDA exemption, revised 5(3)(c), new 8(1B) logo may be omitted
  <=100 cm2, non-retail containers, fortification wording. OFF (excerpts).
- Bold %RDA for added sugar/sat fat/sodium: DRAFT only (17.02.2025). Not in force.
- Front-of-pack warning labels: NOT notified (Supreme Court matter pending, Sep 2026). SEC.

## Verification round 2 (3 Nov 2026), full Version VIII PDF read
- 5(4) symbol size table: confirmed. Not applicable to mineral water, packaged drinking water, carbonated
  water, alcoholic beverages, liquid milk, milk powders, honey.
- 5(10)(a): "'Date of manufacture or packaging' and 'Expiry/Use by' shall be declared on the label. However,
  expression 'Best before' may also be used as optional or additional information." Confirmed.
- 6(3) Table I: confirmed (<=200: 1/2; 200-500: 2/4; 500-2500: 3/5; >2500: 6/8 mm).
- 8(1): threshold = surface area of the PACKAGE <= 100 cm2; exempts ingredients, lot, nutrition, irradiation
  labelling, additives, licence no. AND logo, importer address, instructions - if given on the multi-unit pack.
  8(1)(a): date of manufacture and use-by/expiry not required below 30 cm2 (if on the multi-unit pack).
- 5(14) allergens: gluten cereals, crustacean, milk, egg, fish, peanuts and tree nuts ("Nut"), soy,
  sulphite >= 10 mg/kg. Confirmed.
- Licence number: 14 digits confirmed (FoSCoS: "14-digits license/registration number"). Meaning of the first
  digit: not found in official text, so not checked.
