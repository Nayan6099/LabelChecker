## Verification round 2 (3 Nov 2026, official texts)
- GSR 226(E) 28.03.2022 (in force 1.10.2022), read official
  (consumeraffairs.gov.in/public/upload/files/GSR226_1732707673.pdf, mislabelled "General corrigendum"):
  "(11) The unit sale price in rupees, rounded off to the nearest two decimal place, shall be declared ...
  (i) per gram where net quantity is less than one kilogram and per kilogram where net quantity is more than
  one kilogram; (ii) per centimeter ... per metre; (iii) per mililitre ... per litre; (iv) per number or unit ...
  Provided that for packages containing alcoholic beverages or spirituous liquor, the State Excise Laws ...
  Provided further that declaration of unit sale price is not required ... in which retail sale price is equal
  to the unit sale price."
  Exactly 1 kg / 1 L / 1 m is not covered by the text: LabelCheck accepts either unit there (interpretation).
- GSR 722(E) adds: "declaration of unit sale price is not required for a combination package or a group
  package or a multi-piece package".
- DoCA advisory dated 08.10.2026 (file I-19/50/2022-W&M, Hindi scan): where/when imported goods are labelled
  (Rule 4 as amended by GSR 418(E) of 29.05.2026: AEO Tier 2/3 importers at their premises, others in customs
  bonded warehouses, before clearance). No new label content.
